Privacy Policy

Minimal Charging Screen

This privacy policy applies to Minimal Charging Screen, the Android charging display and screen saver app previously named Minimal ChargeGlow. Its Android package name is com.dci.dev.minimalchargeglow. The core charging display works on your device; optional online services support crash diagnostics, app news, promotional messages, and Google Play purchases.

Effective date / last updated November 2, 2026
Data collection Crash diagnostics when enabled, Firebase messaging identifiers, and in-app campaign interactions. No third-party ads or advertising identifiers; Google Analytics collection is deactivated.
Your choices Local display settings, separate news and offer controls, optional notification permission, and a crash-reporting switch.

Plain-language summary

Minimal Charging Screen does not require an account or sign-in. Charging data, personal notes, appearance settings, and optional charging insights are processed locally. We do not sell personal data or use advertising identifiers. Firebase processes technical diagnostics when crash reporting is enabled, and messaging identifiers and campaign activity when the corresponding message features are enabled. If you email the developer, we receive the email address and information you choose to send.

Information used on your device

  • Battery level, charging status and source, and supported battery readings such as current, voltage, and temperature.
  • Current time, date, time zone, and clock or calendar display preferences. The calendar display does not read your calendar events.
  • Device orientation sensor readings for upright-and-charging behavior.
  • Display preferences, including templates, layout, colors, fonts, brightness, quiet hours, charge targets, OLED protection, and any personal note you enter.
  • If you enable charging insights, measurements from charging displays and a locally saved summary of the last observed session, including times, battery levels, target, and available temperature readings.
  • If you enable the notification indicator and grant Android Notification access, a generic count of eligible active notifications. The app does not extract, save, transmit, or display their content, titles, senders, or app names.

The app does not upload personal notes, charging-session summaries, or display preferences to Firebase as campaign or diagnostic content. Crash reports can include basic device state, such as battery level and orientation, as described below.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Google Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose crashes and application-not-responding errors. Crash reporting is enabled by default. Reports can include stack traces, app version, operating system and device details, basic device state, and installation identifiers. We do not attach personal notes, notification content, names, email addresses, or charging display settings to these reports.

Turn off Send crash reports in More > About to disable automatic reporting. This fully takes effect on the next app launch. Crashlytics may still store reports locally while reporting is disabled; the app requests deletion of unsent reports while the setting is off. This switch does not delete reports already uploaded. The Google Analytics SDK is included for Firebase compatibility, but Analytics collection is permanently deactivated in the app.

App news and promotional messages

You may receive occasional news about app updates and promotional offers, either inside the app or as notifications if you allow them. Opening an offer lets you view more details; it does not make a purchase.

We use Google Firebase to deliver these messages. Firebase processes an identifier for your app installation and technical information needed to provide the service. It also records whether in-app messages are shown, opened, or dismissed. Google Analytics collection remains disabled. Your personal notes, charging history, and the contents of your other notifications are not shared for messaging.

App news and Promotional offers are enabled initially. You can turn either off in More > Notifications & messages, or through Message preferences on the purchase page. You can also manage notification permission in Android settings. Changes may take a little time, so a notification already on its way may still arrive. Turning messages off does not delete information already sent to Firebase.

Notification permission and other system access

  • Post notifications (optional): on supported Android versions, the app explains and requests permission after onboarding, or once after upgrading an existing installation. You can skip or deny it and continue using the app. Revisit More > Notifications & messages > App notifications to request permission or open Android notification settings. News and promotional notification channels can also be managed there. Denying this permission does not by itself disable eligible foreground in-app sheets; use the category controls for that.
  • Notification access (separate and optional): only for the generic charging-display notification indicator described above. This is different from permission to post the app's own notifications. You can revoke Notification access in Android settings.
  • Screen saver / DreamService: lets Android show Minimal Charging Screen as a charging screen saver after you enable it in system settings.
  • Live wallpaper: an optional wallpaper mode, including the lock screen where supported by your device.
  • Device sensors: used locally for upright-device detection when needed.
  • Internet access and Google Play services: used for the online services described in this policy, including messaging, diagnostics, purchases, app updates, and optional font retrieval.

Google Play purchases, updates, and fonts

Google Play Billing handles purchase offers, payment, confirmation, and restoration. The app receives purchase status and purchase tokens to check and acknowledge entitlement, and stores local entitlement and trial information. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Google Play also handles in-app update checks and update installation. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's handling of these services.

The app uses bundled fonts and Android downloadable fonts where available. Downloadable font requests are handled by the Android/Google Play services font provider.

Local storage and retention

Minimal Charging Screen stores preferences, personal notes, trial and entitlement state, charging insights, campaign history, and prompt or permission choices on your device. Local data remains until it is replaced, reset by the relevant feature, or removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Android may transfer eligible app data through device backup or restore, according to your system settings.

To resume a notification you opened, the app may save one campaign's identifier, title, description, type, and tap time in local storage excluded from Android backup. It is eligible for presentation for up to 24 hours, and is cleared when consumed or replaced by a newer tap. Clearing app storage also removes it.

Firebase states that Crashlytics retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. For Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging, installation identifiers are retained until deletion is requested through Firebase; removal from live and backup systems can then take up to 180 days. See Firebase's data-processing and retention information.

Service providers and security

Google processes data for Firebase diagnostics and messaging, Google Play, and supported font services as described above. Firebase uses HTTPS for data in transit and may process data in countries where Google or its service providers operate, including outside your country. See Firebase privacy and security information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices, deletion, and contact

You can change message categories, deny or revoke notification permissions, turn off crash reporting, and disable charging insights. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove local app data. Backups are managed separately through your Android or Google backup settings. These actions do not automatically delete data already sent to Firebase or Google Play. There is no app account to delete.

For privacy questions or requests concerning data already sent to a service provider, contact the developer of Minimal Charging Screen at dci.development.121@gmail.com. If you send feedback or a privacy request by email, your email provider sends us your address and the message or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request.

Children's privacy

Minimal Charging Screen is not directed to children and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please contact the developer if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated to reflect changes to the app or its data practices. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
